About Us

HealthCare for everyone, everywhere

HealthCare was founded with a simple mission: make quality medical guidance accessible to everyone, regardless of where they live or what time it is.

We built an AI that doesn't just answer questions — it listens. It asks the follow-ups a good doctor would ask. It gives you the kind of personalized guidance that used to require an expensive appointment.
